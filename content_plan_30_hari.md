# M2B Logistics Stories — 30-Day Content Plan

**Series:** M2B Logistics Stories — From Documents to Delivery  
**Editorial language:** Indonesian until the founder decides otherwise  
**Purpose:** Build searchable export-import education, demonstrate M2B's operational competence, generate qualified logistics leads, and develop an AdSense-safe content library.

## Fixed Story Formula

1. **Hook:** A realistic shipment problem appears.
2. **Risk:** Show what could happen if the issue is ignored.
3. **Investigation:** The M2B team checks documents, systems, cargo, or vendors.
4. **Decision:** The responsible character chooses a defensible action.
5. **Resolution:** Explain the operational result without making unrealistic guarantees.
6. **Takeaway:** Give readers a practical checklist or prevention step.
7. **CTA:** Invite readers to inspect their shipment readiness or contact M2B.

## Mandatory Editorial Guardrails

- Do not guarantee customs clearance, channel assignment, release time, duties, permits, or inspection outcomes.
- Do not expose real customer identities, document numbers, shipment numbers, credentials, or commercially sensitive rates.
- Distinguish education from case-specific professional advice.
- Use standard Indonesian customs terminology in the published comic; avoid mixed-language interface text.
- Show M2B as an asset-light logistics coordinator and PPJK-related service operator, not as the owner of every truck, vessel, terminal, or warehouse.
- Every problem must end with a practical lesson—not fear-based selling.
- Use AI for drafts and illustrations; a competent human must approve legal, customs, cost, and operational claims.
- AdSense must never interrupt the comic sequence or appear to be part of an M2B button, checklist, or navigation control.

## Founder Decision

Do not publish 30 low-value pages merely to fill 30 calendar days. The plan supplies 30 validated topics, but the recommended production cadence is **four substantial episodes per week plus one weekly pillar/recap**. Use the remaining days for fact-checking, visual QA, internal linking, distribution, and performance review.
